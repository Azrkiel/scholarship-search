## Status pipeline

`FOUND → VETTED → MATERIALS NEEDED → DRAFTING → READY FOR REVIEW → SUBMITTED → RESULT`

Other statuses: `MAYBE` (eligibility unconfirmed — see notes), `CLOSED` (deadline
passed), `INELIGIBLE` (didn't meet criteria — keep the row with a reason, don't delete).
